# Incident Disclosure Mock Report: Flair Airlines ✈️📄

**Course**: CIS3740 – IT Auditing and Risk Management  
**Institution**: Walker College of Business  

This repository contains a simulated incident disclosure report written for the Board of Directors at Flair Airlines, following a cybersecurity breach involving the **AndroxGh0st malware**. The report models how organizations assess and communicate legal and financial impacts of security incidents under various regulatory frameworks.

## 🧾 Overview

- **Incident**: Unauthorized access to Flair Airlines’ web server, compromising sensitive customer data such as names, emails, phone numbers, and flight details.
- **Threat**: Attackers leveraged the AndroxGh0st malware to exploit system vulnerabilities.
- **Response**: The company initiated containment, investigation, and disclosure steps in line with federal compliance expectations.

## ⚖️ Regulatory Frameworks Assessed

- **GLBA (Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act)**: Applicable due to exposure of personal financial information.
- **SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act)**: Evaluated for its implications on financial reporting and shareholder accountability.
- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**: Considered due to Flair Airlines’ interaction with EU citizen data.
- **HIPAA**: Not applicable, but included for contrast and educational purposes.

## 🔍 Key Takeaways

- Flair Airlines may face **legal liability and government penalties** under GLBA and SOX, and possibly GDPR.
- The incident could impact **shareholder trust and financial reporting accuracy**.
- Transparency, prompt incident disclosure (e.g., via Form 8-K), and a commitment to **regulatory compliance** are critical to maintaining stakeholder trust.

## 🛡️ Educational Purpose

This mock report serves as a real-world scenario exercise in IT auditing and cybersecurity analytics. It highlights the importance of legal literacy in cybersecurity, and demonstrates how to:

- Assess corporate exposure under federal law
- Draft incident disclosures for executives and stakeholders
- Identify the role of cybersecurity in governance, risk, and compliance (GRC)
